How it works

Your PC is the station. Your TV tunes in.

Everything stays on your home network. Your videos never leave your house.

Install YokeTV on your PC

Add your movies, shows and home videos. Name episodes like Show Name S01E02 and YokeTV sorts them into shows for you.

Open YokeTV on your Roku

It shows a 6-digit code. Type it on your PC and the two are paired. A quick setup makes profiles for everyone in the house.

Watch

Flip through live channels, pick up where you left off, or browse your shows and movies on demand.

Features

It feels like real TV. It's all yours.

Live TV

Channels that are always on

Make as many stations as you like. Each one plays its lineup around the clock, so turning one on drops you in mid-show, just like cable.

  • Bumpers and commercial breaks you make yourself
  • Up Next and schedule cards between shows
  • Shuffle with two-parters kept together
  • Labels, like a holiday station of everything labeled "holiday"
On your PC

Program it like a TV network

Build each station's lineup, set how often the breaks come, and give it its own watermark in the corner. Change it any time and every TV in the house follows.

On demand

Your whole library, from the couch

Movies, shows by season and home videos, with posters, descriptions and search. Continue Watching remembers where each person stopped.

  • Skip intro button, found by listening to the episodes
  • Next episode as soon as the credits start
  • Subtitles and audio tracks
  • Trailer previews when you rest on a title
Library

Drop in your files. YokeTV does the rest.

Add a folder and YokeTV sorts it into movies and shows. Turn on the free online lookups and it fills in posters, descriptions and ratings too.

Profiles

Everyone gets their own Continue Watching, picture and ratings. Choose what each profile can see, and lock profiles with a PIN.

Ratings

Rate movies and shows from the TV and see what everyone at home thinks, next to the TMDb score.

Make it yours

Pick the accent color, choose what's featured on the home screen, and even style the TV app with CSS.

Every TV in the house

Pair as many Rokus as you like. They all share the same library, stations and profiles.

Updates itself

The PC app finds new versions on its own and installs them when you restart it.

Private by design

No account and no tracking. Your TV only talks to your PC. Read the privacy policy.
