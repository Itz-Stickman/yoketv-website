YokeTV for Roku

Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version
  • No account, no sign-up, no ads.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising IDs. We, the people who make YokeTV, receive nothing from the TV app.
  • Your TV talks only to YokeTV on your own PC, over your home network. Your videos, profiles and watch history stay on your PC.

This policy covers the YokeTV channel for Roku TVs and Roku streaming players (“YokeTV for Roku”, “the app”) and the YokeTV app for Windows it works with (“YokeTV on your PC”). YokeTV for Roku plays the movies, shows, home videos and stations you keep in YokeTV on your PC. It has no videos of its own.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no YokeTV servers for the app to send anything to. It doesn't collect analytics, crash reports, usage statistics, advertising identifiers, your location or your Roku account details, and it shows no ads.

What's kept on your TV

To remember its settings, the app saves a few small things in your TV's storage for this app:

  • The network address and name of the PC it's paired with (for example “192.168.1.20” and “Living Room PC”).
  • A pairing key, which lets YokeTV on your PC know the requests come from your paired TV.
  • Your choices: the accent color, the last profile used, subtitle and player settings, whether trailer previews play, and whether a first-time setup is still going on.

If you enter a profile's PIN, it's kept in the app's memory only until you close the app, and is sent only to your PC to check it.

These never leave your TV, except the pairing key and PIN, which go only to your PC.

What your TV shares with your PC

When you pair, YokeTV on your PC reads your TV's name, model and network address from the TV (as set in Roku settings, for example “Living Room Roku”), so you can tell your TVs apart. After that, the app asks your PC for your library, pictures, stations and the videos themselves, and tells it:

  • which profile is watching, what's playing and where you stopped, for Continue Watching and for the “On your TVs” list on your PC;
  • the ratings you give movies and shows.

All of this goes across your home network only, directly between your TV and your PC, and is stored in YokeTV on your PC.

Your home network carries this traffic without encryption, as is usual for devices on the same Wi-Fi. Use YokeTV on a network you trust. The pairing code shown on your TV and the pairing key keep other devices from using your PC's TV connection.

Services outside your home

YokeTV works without the internet. Some optional features on your PC use online services. They're off until you add your own account or key, and your TV never contacts them:

  • Details and pictures: titles and years of your movies and shows go to TMDb to find posters, descriptions and ratings.
  • Subtitles: a fingerprint of the video file and its title go to OpenSubtitles to find matching subtitles, with your OpenSubtitles login if you add one.
  • Watch history: if you connect Trakt, what the main profile watches is sent to your Trakt account.
  • Updates: the Windows app checks GitHub for new versions of YokeTV.

Intro and credits detection and the trailers YokeTV makes all happen on your PC. Plugins you choose to add to YokeTV on your PC run there and could use the internet; only add plugins you trust. Each online service has its own privacy policy.

Roku

Your Roku device and Roku's services collect information under Roku's Privacy Policy, separately from YokeTV. YokeTV doesn't receive any of it.

Children

YokeTV for Roku isn't directed at children under 13, and since it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either. Parents can make a profile that only sees what they choose, and lock other profiles with a PIN.

Removing your information

  • To unpair, choose Options → Disconnect on the TV's home screen. This deletes the saved address and pairing key from your TV.
  • Removing the YokeTV channel from your Roku deletes everything the app saved on your TV.
  • On your PC, Connect TV → Remove forgets a TV. Profiles, watch history and ratings live in YokeTV on your PC, where you can change or delete them.

Changes

If a later version handles information differently, we'll update this page and the date above before that version comes out. If a change means the app would collect information about you, we'll say so plainly here first.
