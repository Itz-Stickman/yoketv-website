What's new

Every version of YokeTV for Windows and Roku. The Windows app updates itself; you'll see a Restart to update button when a new one is ready.

0.13.0LatestFirst-time setup and a welcome tour2026-11-25

New

  • First-time setup. A new install walks you through three steps:
    • Type the code from your TV (or connect one later)
    • Make your profile, and one for everyone else at home
    • Pick your accent color
  • Welcome tour. After the setup, a short tour shows where to add videos, how to edit a movie, show or episode, how to make a station and how to connect more TVs. See it again any time in Customize → Welcome tour.
  • On the TV: while you're setting up, your TV shows Continue the setup on [your PC], in the color you're picking, and carries on by itself when you're done.

Fixed

  • Setting a profile PIN on the PC always said "A PIN is 4 numbers", even when it was.
0.12.0Automatic updates2026-11-25

New

  • YokeTV updates itself. It checks for a new version when it starts and every hour after. Updates download in the background. When one is ready, the sidebar shows Restart to update and Windows shows a notification. If you don't restart, it installs the next time YokeTV closes.
  • Choose how often it checks, or check now, in Customize → Updates.
0.1 – 0.11The first builds2026-11-22 – 11-25

Stations

  • Your own TV stations that play around the clock, with your own bumpers, commercial breaks, and Up Next and schedule cards between shows.
  • The YokeTV channel, built in, plays your whole library. Leave things off it, or give them labels to make stations like a holiday channel.
  • Shuffle the lineup, play each show's episodes in random order, and keep two-part episodes together.
  • Trailers between programs: YokeTV makes them from your videos, or use your own.
  • A watermark in the corner of each station: its name, your own picture, or none.

Watching

  • Movies, shows by season and home videos on demand, with search and Continue Watching.
  • Skip intro, found by listening to the episodes, and the next episode starts when the credits do. You can also set where an episode really ends.
  • Trailer previews on the TV's home screen.
  • Subtitles and audio tracks, with subtitle size, color and background.

Profiles and ratings

  • Profiles with their own picture, Continue Watching and choice of what they can see. Open straight into one, or ask who's watching.
  • PIN lock for profiles.
  • Ratings from 1 to 5 for movies and shows, set from the TV or the PC, next to TMDb's score.

Online (optional)

  • Posters, descriptions and title logos from TMDb, subtitles from OpenSubtitles, and watch history with Trakt, using your own free accounts.

Make it yours

  • Accent color, the featured spot on the TV's home screen, which rows show, TV CSS, and plugins.
